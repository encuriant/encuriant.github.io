What would happen if the earth were to stop rotating and revolving?
3 min read
Published on Oct 7, 2026

When such a tremendous event strikes, many bizarre first-order and second-order effects will come into play, especially because of physics—–and also because of the society—, so I have carefully analyzed this hypothetical from many angles to construct the essay beneath.

First and foremost, besides everyone dying (due to physics), the clocks will stop running, both digital and analog, and also nearly all sundials, which means, the times as we know it will cease to exist. As if that shock was not enough, in its aftermath, timezones will collapse; sounds terrifying, but sociologically, it will be viewed as a great relief by two societies: US civilians who cannot tell which way the clock rotates with daylight saving time, and the grammar officials who cannot stand anyone calling it “daylight savings”.

Now, assuming no one dies so that we have more to critically study, critique and articulate on our plate, observe that many nations will be stuck in forever-day and many others in forever-night. This will inflict unprecedented disorientation upon everybody’s biology clock and cicada rhythm. But the sliver lining: the few hundreds populating the poles will do just fine as they have been enduring such fates in alternation since 0AD, so biologically speaking, it’s just a Tuesday or an August for them. More fortunately, the Antarticians are an ensemble of science experts in laboratories, which means they saw this all coming with their telescopes and their periscopes and so, were pre-prepared; and thankfully nobody is on the Arctic because it’s all thawed now.

If we refocus our analysis onto the societological rather than the scientological effects, we are faced with the eye-opening fact that one half of the global sphere will no longer need to wake up for work. Ironically, this will be a wake-up call for the national leaders (the prime ministers and the presidents and the oil and AI company executives) who will call and discuss this dire situation at once in the Alps or Versailles; they will cooperate in a mutually beneficial manner and reach an agreement that masterfully steers the economy and the planet it subsists upon, preventing in one hemisphere a recession, and on the other hemisphere, whatever is the opposite of a recession, which is either inflation or GDP. This sort of econometrics is beyond the scope of this essay, so the reader must tread here with caution.

A question must be really bothering the keen reader at this point: what happens to the solar eclipses? They will continue to happen—but only for the working people in Day Nation, which will of course be rewarding for the Day persons, assuming the Day Billionaires deem it okay for the workerly class to take a break from work—this may well be part of the economic treaty aforementioned. On the other hemisphere, the Night persons must be content with the boring Lunar eclipse, but the Night persons are fortunately mostly asleep.

What’s really depressing though about the Night nations is their plants (or “flora”, which is how good writing refers to it): the Night nation florae will all perish (biologically speaking) except for the “zz” plant which multiple plant shop owners have advised and advertised as apt for “part to no light” conditions. This too lies out of scope for this essay for the reason that I don’t own a plant shop.

But let me terminate this essay with an optimism: this is all actually exciting for the planet because there becomes possible a new form of tourism where the Night people who are unable to sleep (hopefully enough of them, from all the terror and trauma) will take planes and yachts to visit Day nations and gawk at their lush green floras and their foliages (i.e., the literary version of leaves) and vice versa. This, and the many other anti-recession opportunities that I can envision, will be enshrined in the The First Economic Treaty of the Day-Night Hemispheres, which should last at least a “day” before The First Day-Night war to end all wars breaks out and everyone dies.
