About

I’m a scientist by professional training, but unprofessionally, I experiment with a few different things, one of which is to write sentences, particularly long sentences, sentences about rabbit holes I burrowed into, sentences about words and languages, sentences describing graphically my sensory experiences, sentences about art and music (particularly Indian music and my approach to appreciating it), sentences observing nature, the universe, humans, their mind and their senses, and counteracting all that, sentences that are nonsense. I believe in approaching the complex universe around us and within us with a scientific and artistic mind, a noticing eye, a curious nose, a creative left hand and moisturized skin. I believe that being idle, being alone and being a pedestrian (in the literal, not figurative sense) is the laboratory where the most violent sparks of creativity and insight are synthesized, sparks which I hope to keep note of here. This is what all the pioneering scientists and thinkers, the Einsteins and the Wittgensteins believed in and tell us, but now you’ve heard it also from a pioneering overthinker.

But what is an encuriant?When you become curious about an inanimate object, if one were to turn the scenario upside down and animate the object of curiosity, what action has that object performed on you? Provoke? Stimulate? Arouse? All those verbs stem from a different root adulterated with spurious connotations. No verb conjures purely the image of an object with agency planting in you one question, and that question leading to another, and question after question rolling in, luring you closer and closer towards the object like a complex fragrance leading you deviously into the center of a labyrinth. What the object of your curiosity has done is to encuriate you. The world, for the curious and the encuriable, is filled with such agents of curiosity, the encuriants.What I have done here is to shape language by paralleling the form of “furious –> infuriate” to create “curious –> encuriate”. It would have been more standard to go with incuriate but it sounded more like a derogatory adjective that means “not being curious” rather than a verb that brings forth an action. This is like how the word “donate” was back-invented from the word “donation” (not the other way around!)By making you ask “what is an encuriant?”, the word “encuriant” becomes an autological wordAn autological word is a word whose definition describes itself, like the words noun, English, pentasyllabic, and also the word, word.You have reached the center of this labyrinth… because it encuriated you.
